The craft

Every cup starts as a green bean and a decision.

We source directly from growers in Ethiopia, Colombia and Sumatra, then roast in small drums until each origin hits its own sweet spot. No shortcuts, no burnt corners — just coffee that tastes like the place it came from.

Rotate the cup. That warmth is real; the steam is math.
