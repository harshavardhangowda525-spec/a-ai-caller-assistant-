Our Story

A neighbourhood coffee house built on good beans, better people, and the belief that every cup is worth celebrating.

How it began

From a market stall to a morning ritual

In the spring of 2024, two friends — a barista and a baker — set up a single cart under a string of lanterns at the Old Town market. We had one espresso machine, a hand grinder, and a stubborn idea: that coffee should feel like an occasion, not a transaction.

Word travelled fast. Within a year that cart became this café — a warm, light-filled room where regulars linger, strangers become neighbours, and every drink is still made by hand, with the same care we poured into cup number one.

🌱 Ethical sourcing

Good coffee should be good for everyone

We buy direct from smallholder farms in Ethiopia, Colombia and Guatemala, paying well above Fair Trade minimums. Every green bean is traceable to the family that grew it. We roast in small batches, compost our grounds, and serve in reusable and home-compostable ware — because a celebration shouldn’t cost the earth.
